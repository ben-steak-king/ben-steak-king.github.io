Spice up your events with old-school Italian charm on Hollywood Road

The Lasagna Factory on 208 Hollywood Road is a cozy Italian restaurant and bar located on ‘The World’s 2nd Coolest Street’ (TimeOut). Housed in a former meat warehouse, it features a bar downstairs and two dining rooms — one seating 50 and another seating 80 guests upstairs.

The 4,000 square foot venue holds up to 200 seated or 250 standing guests. Choose from the downstairs bar, private rooms, or the upstairs dining area. Ideal for:

  • Private Parties & Celebrations
  • Group Lunches & Dinners
  • Corporate Events
  • Product Launches
  • Press Events
  • Wine Tastings

We keep party bookings simple and affordable with a fantastic menu for large parties at HK$288 per person. Enjoy delicious sides and shareable mains, all served family-style. For dessert, indulge in our classic Italian treats. Want to elevate your meal? Add our signature porterhouse or tomahawk steak for $888.

Enhance the experience with our Free Flow Drinks Package — Prosecco, wine, craft beer, and soft drinks for 2 or 3 hours, making your celebration effortless and enjoyable.

A casual downstairs bar with long communal tables, checkered tablecloths, and a New York Italian spirit. Enjoy drinks at the entrance or sit inside for a hearty plate of pasta.

The ground floor private room features warm lighting, its own washrooms, and a wall of wine. Configurable for up to 50 guests with round or long tables — ideal for meetings and intimate dinners.

Upstairs, discover rich wooden floors and a pizza oven crafted in Naples. This secluded dining space offers fragrant aromas, privacy, and a view of Hong Kong's bustling street life.

The Lasagna Factory pays homage to second-generation Italian diners in New York — with giant spicy meatballs, thick lasagna, and bold homemade sauces. Everything is served family-style.
